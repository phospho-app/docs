Making your robot come to life, on its own is the next step in your robotics journey.
Learn how to train AI models with your robot arm and make it perform tasks autonomously.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, make sure you have the following:
- Your phospho junior dev kit set up
- A way to teleoperate your robot arm (we recommend using our [teleoperation app](/mintlify/examples/teleop))

# ACT

This page is a work in progress. Find all the information you need to get started with ACT in our phosphobot repo [here](https://github.com/phospho-app/phosphobot).

1. Create a training dataset by teleoperating the robot arm to perform the desired task. (aim for ~50 episodes)
2. Train an ACT model using your dataset.
3. Use the inference script to run the trained model on the robot arm.
